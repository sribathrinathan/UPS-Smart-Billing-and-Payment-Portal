UPS SMART BILLING & PAYMENT PORTAL
COMPLETE PROJECT DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This document is the complete product and technical specification for building
the UPS Smart Billing & Payment Portal hackathon project.

IMPORTANT:
- Build a complete, working full-stack application, not just a UI mockup.
- The mandatory problem-statement features must work first.
- AI is encouraged by the problem statement but is NOT mandatory.
- AI should be used only where it adds meaningful business value.
- The project should feel specific to UPS and logistics billing rather than a
  generic invoice CRUD application.
- Use realistic demo data and clearly label simulated/sandbox functionality.
- Do not claim that the application is an official UPS internal system.
- Do not use real customer/payment data.

============================================================
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
============================================================

COMPANY:
United Parcel Service (UPS)

USE CASE:
Smart Billing & Payment Portal

GOAL:
For a logistics company, build a Smart Billing & Payment Portal that enables
customers to view invoices, make payments, track payment status and receive
billing insights.

Finance users need a dashboard showing:
- Total invoices
- Paid invoices
- Pending invoices
- Overdue invoices
- Collection summary
- Revenue trend

The problem statement encourages teams to leverage:
- Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI
- Conversational interfaces and chatbots
- Predictive analytics
- Personalization engines
- Mobile-first experiences
- Cloud-native architectures
- API-driven integrations

AI IS OPTIONAL:
The application must remain useful and fully functional even if the AI layer
is unavailable. Core billing, invoice, payment and finance workflows must be
implemented using normal application/business logic.

============================================================
2. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

Do NOT build only:
"An invoice management website."

Build:
"An intelligent predictive billing platform for a logistics company that
connects shipments to invoices, explains billing changes, predicts shipment
costs, detects potential billing problems before invoice finalization, helps
finance teams prioritize collections, and provides a billing-focused AI
copilot."

CORE IDEA:

Traditional billing:
Shipment -> Invoice -> Payment

Proposed system:
Shipment
   ->
Billing/Rate Engine
   ->
Validation + Analytics + AI
   ->
Invoice
   ->
Payment
   ->
Payment Reconciliation
   ->
Insights / Predictions / Recommended Actions

============================================================
11. AI INVOICE DISPUTE PREVENTION
============================================================

Do not wait for the customer to dispute an invoice.

Before invoice finalization:

Shipment data
    ->
Rate/contract information
    ->
Charge calculation
    ->
Validation
    ->
Anomaly detection
    ->
Decision

No anomaly:
Generate invoice

Anomaly:
Review required

Example:

Potential Billing Issue

Shipment: 1Z999...

Contracted rate: Rs. 4,200
Calculated rate: Rs. 5,760
Difference: Rs. 1,560

Reason:
Additional handling charge detected.

Action:
Review before invoice generation.

The system should record:
- anomaly type
- expected value
- actual value
- deviation
- severity
- explanation
- status
- reviewer
- resolution

============================================================
12. UNEXPECTED CHARGE DETECTION
============================================================

Detect unusual billing behaviour.

Examples:
- Current charge significantly above historical average
- Unexpected customs charge
- Unexpected handling fee
- Duplicate charge
- Duplicate shipment reference
- Abnormal discount
- Incorrect tax
- Rate mismatch

Example:

UNUSUAL CHARGE DETECTED

Expected customs charge: Rs. 18,000
Actual customs charge: Rs. 42,700
Deviation: +137%

Possible reasons:
- Shipment classification changed
- Additional brokerage charge
- New customs service

Use deterministic anomaly rules first.
Optional ML model can be added later.

============================================================
13. PREDICTIVE FINAL COST
============================================================

Before final billing, estimate the expected shipment cost.

Potential inputs:
- Origin
- Destination
- Weight
- Dimensions
- Service type
- Fuel surcharge
- Customs
- Additional services
- Historical charges
- Customer-specific billing patterns

Example:

Predicted final cost: Rs. 13,170
Actual final cost: Rs. 13,420
Variance: Rs. 250

Possible ML approaches:
- Linear Regression for simple baseline
- Random Forest
- Gradient Boosting
- XGBoost if available and justified

For the hackathon, a simple explainable model is preferable to an unnecessarily
complex model.

Show prediction factors where possible.

============================================================
14. PAYMENT RISK PREDICTION
============================================================

For unpaid invoices, calculate a payment risk score.

Possible inputs:
- Previous payment delays
- Average payment delay
- Outstanding amount
- Invoice amount
- Days until due date
- Days overdue
- Number of previous overdue invoices
- Dispute frequency
- Customer payment consistency

Output:

Payment Risk: HIGH
Risk score: 87/100

Reasons:
- 4 previous late payments
- Average delay: 28 days
- Rs. 9.2L currently outstanding
- Current invoice is 42% higher than average
- Due date approaching

Important:
Show reasons. Do not provide only a black-box percentage.

2. DISPUTE REDUCTION
Potential billing issues are identified before finalization.

Finance teams know which invoices/customers deserve attention first.

The system predicts costs, risks and collection scenarios rather than only
reporting historical information.
